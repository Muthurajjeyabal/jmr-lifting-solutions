Eight engineering services, one clean handoff.

JMR is an independent lifting engineering desk. Every service below produces a defensible document your project team, your contractor, your client, and your regulator can all sign against.

Quality Guarantee · Every Service

All lifts planned and engineered under the direction of a LEEA Certified Appointed Person — compliant with BS 7121 and ASME B30 standards. Every deliverable listed below is produced under LOLER 1998 planning duty-of-care and audited against international heavy-lift & rigging engineering codes.

SERVICE · 01

Lift Planning

Every critical lift, planned to the millimetre.

We produce complete lift plans covering rigging design, crane selection, load-radius verification, ground bearing pressure, sling angles, and step-by-step execution sequence — for single-crane, tandem, and complex multi-crane lifts.

Deliverables
  • Full lift plan document
  • Sling & rigging register
  • Crane load charts verified for radius
  • Step-by-step execution sequence
  • Rigging arrangement drawings
When you need it

Vessel installation · Reactor changeout · Module lift · Turbine lift · Any lift above 40 t

SERVICE · 02

Method Statements

The document that becomes the operation.

Detailed written method statements that describe every phase of the operation — pre-lift checks, rigging, crane positioning, primary lift, translation, set-down, and de-rigging — with roles, responsibilities and hold-points clearly defined.

Deliverables
  • Phase-by-phase method statement
  • Roles & responsibilities matrix
  • Hold-points and stop-work criteria
  • Communication protocol
  • Emergency response procedure
When you need it

Client requires a formal MS · Contractor onboarding · Regulator or client review

SERVICE · 03

Risk Assessment

Every hazard identified before the crane arrives.

Structured risk assessments covering mechanical, structural, environmental, and operational hazards. Each risk scored, ranked, mitigated to ALARP, and signed off.

Deliverables
  • Full risk register
  • Hazard identification matrix
  • Mitigation controls per hazard
  • Residual risk score
  • ALARP demonstration
When you need it

High-risk lifts · Confined spaces · Live plants · Adjacent to occupied buildings

SERVICE · 04

Engineering Calculations

The maths behind the lift, transparent and defensible.

Structural and rigging calculations — sling tension, spreader beam design, lifting lug capacity, trunnion analysis, ground bearing pressure, crane utilisation — presented as auditable calculation reports.

Deliverables
  • Sling tension calculations (per phase)
  • Spreader beam / lifting frame design
  • Lifting lug / trunnion check
  • Ground bearing pressure analysis
  • Crane utilisation (SWL vs. actual)
When you need it

Custom rigging · Non-standard lifts · Client requires stamped calculations

SERVICE · 05

Lifting Procedure Review

A second set of engineering eyes before you sign.

Independent technical review of a lift plan or procedure that another party has produced — for the client, the contractor, or the regulator. We identify gaps, technical errors, and areas of unresolved risk.

Deliverables
  • Technical review report
  • Findings register with severity
  • Recommended corrections
  • Sign-off statement (if suitable)
When you need it

Client hires main contractor · Regulator requires 3rd-party review · Post-incident learning

SERVICE · 06

Lift Plan Audit

Confidence that the plan on paper is the plan on site.

Structured audit of an existing lift plan against LEEA, LOLER, DNV-ST-N001, API RP 2D, ASME B30, and BS 7121 requirements. Complete gap analysis with actionable findings.

Deliverables
  • Full audit report
  • Compliance checklist per standard
  • Non-conformance register
  • Corrective action plan
When you need it

Client compliance check · Insurance requirement · Pre-mobilisation review

Deliverables
