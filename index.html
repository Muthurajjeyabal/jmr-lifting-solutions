02 — What we do

When a component weighs 1,800 tonnes and cannot fail — we are the team that engineers the moment.

JMR Lifting Solutions is a specialist heavy-lift and engineered transport contractor. We plan, engineer, and execute the world's most complex lifts — refinery reactor changeouts, offshore module installations, tandem upending of pressure vessels, and turnkey transport of oversized industrial components.

Every project begins with engineering, not equipment. Load paths, ground bearing pressures, wind envelopes, and rigging geometry are resolved in our engineering office before a single crane ever rolls onto site.

03 — Capabilities

Four disciplines. One standard.

01

Tandem & Multi-crane Upending

Coordinated dual-crane vertical erection of pressure vessels, columns, and reactors up to 1,800 tonnes. Fully engineered load path from horizontal to vertical.

  • Crawler + mobile tandem lifts
  • Trunnion & tailing rigging design
  • Real-time load monitoring
02

Engineered Heavy Transport

SPMT-based transport of oversized loads through refineries, ports, and constrained sites. Route surveys, ground preparation, and clearance engineering included.

  • Self-propelled modular transporters
  • Route & bridge load analysis
  • Ro-Ro and quayside operations
03

Engineered Rigging Design

In-house rigging engineering — from lift plans and pad-eye design to spreader beams and custom lifting frames. FEA-verified, insurer-accepted, code-compliant.

  • ASME B30 & BS 7121 compliant
  • Custom spreader & lifting frames
  • 3D lift simulation & clash detection
04

Site Assessment & Method Statements

Pre-project assessments, ground bearing studies, and method statements accepted by tier-1 EPC contractors and international insurers.

  • Ground bearing & crane pad design
  • Method statements & HIRA
  • Insurer & MWS liaison
