# JMR Lifting — Sample Lift Plans (10 Scenarios)

All plans follow BS 7121, LOLER 1998, ASME B30 standards. Compiled under LEEA Appointed Person responsibility.

## The 10 Plans

### 1. Heavy Vessel Lift — Refinery Reactor
**Scope:** Tandem lift of hydrocracker reactor
**Load:** 285 tonnes, 42 m long × 5.2 m dia
**Location:** Refinery / process plant
**Cranes:** 2 × Liebherr LR 1600/2 crawler (600 t)
**Configuration:** Tandem lift, symmetric spread
**Key challenge:** COG shift during upending

### 2. Wind Turbine Nacelle Installation
**Scope:** Onshore wind farm nacelle placement
**Load:** 82 tonnes nacelle at 130 m hub height
**Location:** Wind farm (greenfield)
**Crane:** Liebherr LR 11000 crawler (1000 t)
**Configuration:** Main boom + luffing jib, 176 m tip height
**Key challenge:** Wind speed monitoring, narrow tower flange access

### 3. Elevated Storage Tank Placement — 100m Height
**Scope:** Water storage tank on structural tower
**Load:** 45 tonnes tank shell (empty)
**Location:** Industrial facility
**Crane:** Liebherr LTM 1750-9.1 mobile (750 t)
**Configuration:** Main boom + fixed jib, 108 m working height
**Key challenge:** Load radius vs boom length optimization

### 4. Large Industrial Generator Installation
**Scope:** Combined cycle plant generator placement
**Load:** 195 tonnes generator + stator
**Location:** Power plant, indoor installation
**Crane:** Liebherr LR 1750/2 crawler (750 t)
**Configuration:** Main boom lift through roof opening
**Key challenge:** Overhead clearance, precise positioning ±10 mm

### 5. Offshore Platform Module Lift
**Scope:** Living quarters module onto jacket
**Load:** 380 tonnes LQ module (18 m × 22 m × 8 m)
**Location:** Offshore fixed platform (West Africa)
**Vessel/Crane:** HLV Saipem 7000 (semi-sub, 2 × 7000 t)
**Configuration:** Single vessel lift, 4-point spreader
**Key challenge:** Weather window, dynamic amplification factor

### 6. Bridge Girder Erection
**Scope:** Highway overpass steel girder placement
**Load:** 65 tonnes × 48 m span
**Location:** Live traffic corridor
**Cranes:** 2 × Liebherr LTM 1500-8.1 mobile (500 t)
**Configuration:** Tandem lift over roadway
**Key challenge:** Traffic management, night work window

### 7. Subsea Christmas Tree Installation
**Scope:** Deepwater XT deployment
**Load:** 78 tonnes XT + spanner joint
**Location:** Offshore drilling rig
**Vessel/Crane:** Rig crane 100 t + moonpool deployment
**Configuration:** Vertical launch through moonpool
**Key challenge:** Splash zone, dynamic loading, current profile

### 8. Substation Transformer Placement
**Scope:** 400 kV power transformer installation
**Load:** 340 tonnes transformer + fluid
**Location:** Grid substation
**Crane:** Liebherr LG 1750 lattice truck (750 t)
**Configuration:** Main boom, SL2DB configuration
**Key challenge:** Foundation contact loading, rail transport interface

### 9. LNG Storage Tank Roof Air-Raise
**Scope:** Aluminium inner roof of LNG tank
**Load:** 220 tonnes roof assembly
**Location:** LNG terminal
**Method:** Air-raise (not crane lift) with backup crane
**Backup crane:** Liebherr LR 1400/2 (400 t)
**Key challenge:** Pressure differential control, roof guidance

### 10. Pressure Vessel Tandem Lift & Turn
**Scope:** Horizontal-to-vertical rotation of column
**Load:** 165 tonnes distillation column
**Location:** Petrochemical plant
**Cranes:** Main: Liebherr LTM 1500-8.1 (500 t) + Tail: LTM 1200-5.1 (200 t)
**Configuration:** Tandem lift, tail crane trolleys during upending
**Key challenge:** Trigonometry of tail crane travel, load transfer point
