Standards & best practices

The frameworks behind our work.

We do not claim third-party certification we do not hold. What we do promise: every document we produce is engineered against the internationally recognised lifting engineering codes below.

“JMR Lifting produces engineering documents that align with the codes of practice used by insurers, regulators, and major clients worldwide. Certification is a service we recommend for equipment; discipline is what we bring to the paperwork.”

Frameworks we reference
  • LOLER 1998Lifting Operations & Lifting Equipment Regulations
  • LEEA CoPSULECode of Practice for the Safe Use of Lifting Equipment
  • DNV-ST-N001Marine operations and marine warranty
  • DNV-RP-H103Modelling and analysis of marine operations
  • API RP 2DOperation & maintenance of offshore cranes
  • ASME B30Safety standard for cranes, hoists, and rigging
  • BS 7121Code of practice for safe use of cranes
